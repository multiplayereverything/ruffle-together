# Games

These games have a key map built in. Start them with `--coop-keys <name>` (the launcher does it for you).

| Game | Players | Key map |
|---|---|---|
| Bad Ice-Cream | 2 | `bad-ice-cream` (the default) |
| Gun Mayhem 2 | 2 to 4 | `gun-mayhem-2` |
| Territory War | 2 to 4 (taking turns) | `territory-war` |
| Fireboy & Watergirl in the Forest Temple | 2 | `fireboy-watergirl` |
| Bow Man | 2 | `bow-man` |
| Smileys War | 2 to 4 | `smileys-war` |
| Stealth Assassin | 2 to 4 | `stealth-assassin` |
| Stealth Hunter 2 | 2 to 4 | `stealth-hunter-2` |
| Intrusion | 2 to 4 | `intrusion` |
| Intrusion 2 | 2 to 4 | `intrusion-2` |
| Air Battle | 2 to 4 | `air-battle` |
| Ultimate Crab Battle | 2 to 4 | `ultimate-crab-battle` |
| Chaos Faction 2 | 2 to 6 (3 to 6 need its 6-player SWF) | `chaos-faction-2` (each player picks their own character card) |

Some of these are co-op mods of single-player games: use the mod's SWF, not the original.

## Other games

- A game with local multiplayer can bring its own key map as a small text file:
  `--coop-keys my-game.keys`. See [Key maps](../make/key-maps.md).
- A game made for Ruffle Together can set its own key map from inside the game, so it needs no `--coop-keys` at
  all (Dad 'n Me does this).
